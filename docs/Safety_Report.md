# Safety Report: Clinical Defense-in-Depth & Risk Mitigation

**Document Version:** 2.5.0-PROD  
**Principal Engineers:** Principal AI Safety Engineer & Clinical Software Architect  
**Evaluation Scope:** Adversarial Red-Teaming, Jailbreaks, Medical Triage, and Harm Reduction  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Clinical AI deployments in vernacular languages face unique safety risks:
1. **Adversarial Jailbreaks in Low-Resource Dialects:** Attackers often bypass English safety filters by transliterating harmful prompts into Telugu or Tanglish.
2. **Delayed Emergency Escalation:** Misinterpreting acute symptoms (chest pain, acute stroke FAST signs) as routine symptoms leads to patient morbidity or mortality.
3. **Unsanctioned Medication Tampering:** Patients frequently ask AI if they can double their antihypertensive dose or discontinue insulin.

The **VastCare Safety Engine** implements a deterministic, multi-layer defense-in-depth shield that intercepts queries **before** generation and wraps responses with localized legal disclaimers.

## 4. Medication-Risk & Dosage Modification Defense

When users query self-directed alterations to prescription pharmaceuticals:
- The system flags `MEDICATION_RISK_DETECTED`.
- It emits a bold clinical warning: modifying prescription schedules without physician consultation causes fatal hypoglycemic collapse, rebound hypertensive crisis, or organ damage.
- Prohibits recommending dose escalations or de-escalations.

## 5. Disclaimer Enforcement

Every single clinical response generated by the system is mandatorily appended with the official VastCare Clinical Disclaimer:

> *వైద్య నిరాకరణ (Vastcare Clinical Disclaimer)*: ఈ సమాచారం కేవలం అధికారిక మార్గదర్శకాల ఆధారంగా రూపొందించబడిన ఆరోగ్య అవగాహన కొరకు మాత్రమే. ఇది వైద్యుడి వ్యక్తిగత రోగ నిర్ధారణ లేదా చికిత్సకు ప్రత్యామ్నాయం కాదు. అత్యవసర పరిస్థితుల్లో తక్షణమే 108 కు కాల్ చేయండి.
