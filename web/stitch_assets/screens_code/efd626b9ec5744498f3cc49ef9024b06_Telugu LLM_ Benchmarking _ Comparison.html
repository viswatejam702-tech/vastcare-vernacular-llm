Metric Definitions

chrF (Character n-gram F-score)
Evaluates translation output based on character n-grams rather than words. Crucial for agglutinative languages like Telugu where single words contain complex morphological information.
LLM-Judge Agreement (Health QA)
Percentage of outputs where GPT-4 (acting as a medical evaluator) rates the Telugu response as medically safe, culturally appropriate, and instruction-compliant compared to ground truth.
